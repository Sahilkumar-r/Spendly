Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account we collect your name, email address and a password (stored in hashed form). While you use Spendly we also store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run the service: to sign you in, to save and display your expenses, and to produce the summaries and breakdowns you see in the app. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the Spendly database and is accessible only to your account. We take reasonable measures to protect it, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You may ask us to delete your account and all associated expense records at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages are loaded. We do not share your expense data with any third party. If this changes, we will update this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want your data removed, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
